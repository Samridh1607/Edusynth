Effective date: 1 June 2025
EduSynth ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This statement explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights over it.

1. Who We Are

EduSynth is an AI-powered educational platform developed and operated by EduSynth Ltd., headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab, India. For privacy enquiries, contact us at privacy@edusynth.ai.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data — full name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash).
  • Usage data — task selections, interaction logs, session duration, and feature usage patterns.
  • Uploaded content — documents, question banks, or syllabi you upload to use platform features.
  • Device & technical data — browser type, operating system, IP address, and cookies (see Cookie Policy).

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide, personalise, and improve EduSynth features.
  • Process your AI-powered task requests via Azure AI services.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent fraud.
  • Send service-related communications (no marketing without consent).
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under one or more of the following bases: contractual necessity (to deliver the service), legitimate interests (security, analytics), and consent (optional cookies and communications).

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Microsoft Azure — cloud hosting and AI inference services, under a Data Processing Agreement.
  • Analytics providers — aggregated, anonymised usage data only.
  • Legal authorities — when required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained for the duration of your account plus 30 days after deletion. Uploaded documents are deleted from our servers within 24 hours of processing unless you explicitly save them. Anonymised usage analytics are retained for up to 2 years.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to act.

To exercise any right, email privacy@edusynth.ai. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards including TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, role-based access controls, and regular security audits.

9. Changes to This Statement

We may update this Privacy Statement periodically. We will notify registered users by email at least 14 days before material changes take effect. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the revised statement.

10. Children's Data

If you are under 18, you may use EduSynth only with the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian, as required by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We do not use children's data for tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising.

11. Grievance Redressal

If you have a concern about how we handle your personal data, contact our Grievance Officer at privacy@edusynth.ai. We will acknowledge your complaint promptly and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India or, if you are in the EU or UK, to your local data protection supervisory authority.
