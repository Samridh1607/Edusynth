What is EduSynth?

An AI workspace for teaching material

EduSynth reads the books and syllabi you already teach from and helps you create knowledge from them. It writes questions, builds question papers and marking schemes, compares textbooks, and shows where your assessments miss parts of the syllabus.

Teachers spend less time on preparation and more time teaching. Students get practice material that matches what they are actually studying.
