Cookie Policy

What cookies we use and how you can control them

Effective date: 1 June 2025
Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit EduSynth. This policy explains what cookies we use, why, and how to manage your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies allow our platform to remember your actions and preferences over time, so you don't have to re-enter them whenever you return. They can be set by EduSynth ("first-party") or by our service providers ("third-party").

2. Cookies We Use

EssentialSession & Authentication Cookies

Required for the platform to function. They keep you logged in during your session and protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF). These cannot be disabled.

  • edu_session — maintains your authenticated session (expires on browser close).
  • edu_csrf_token — security token to prevent CSRF attacks (session-scoped).

AnalyticsUsage & Performance Cookies

Help us understand how users interact with the platform so we can improve it. All data is aggregated and anonymised.

  • edu_analytics_id — anonymised user identifier for session analytics (expires 1 year).
  • edu_perf — captures page load times and error rates (expires 30 days).

FunctionalPreference Cookies

Remember your interface preferences such as language, theme, and last-used task category.

  • edu_prefs — stores UI preferences (expires 1 year).

3. Third-Party Cookies

We use Microsoft Azure Application Insights for telemetry. Azure may set its own cookies governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement. No advertising or social-media tracking cookies are used.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies via Settings → Privacy. Note: disabling essential cookies will break sign-in functionality.
  • Cookie consent banner — on your first visit, you can accept or decline non-essential cookies.
  • Opt-out tools — for analytics, you can opt out via our in-app settings at any time.

5. Do Not Track

EduSynth honours browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. When DNT is enabled, we disable all analytics and functional cookies automatically.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technology or regulation. The effective date at the top will be updated accordingly. For questions, contact privacy@edusynth.ai.
